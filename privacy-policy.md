# Privacy Policy — SipSplit

**Last updated: April 27, 2026**

## 1. Overview
SipSplit ("the App") is a drink tracking and bill splitting app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## 2. Data We Collect
SipSplit stores all data **locally on your device only**. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

The following data is stored locally on your device:
- Party session names and drink logs
- Friend names you enter manually
- Payment handles (PayPal, Revolut, Venmo, Wise, IBAN)
- App preferences (currency, theme, language)

## 3. Camera and Photo Library
The App requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to add photos to your party sessions. These photos are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

## 4. Advertising
The free version of SipSplit displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

## 5. In-App Purchases
SipSplit offers a one-time purchase ("Party Pass") processed by Google Play. We do not store any payment information. Please refer to [Google Play's Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) for details.

## 6. Third-Party Services
- **Google AdMob** — advertising
- **Google Firebase** — crash reporting and analytics
- **RevenueCat** — purchase management

## 7. Children's Privacy
SipSplit is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
